# Medical Certificates and Forms

## Medical Certificates

A medical certificate costs $30 and needs a consultation. A doctor can backdate a certificate by 1 day at most.

## Insurance and Employer Forms

We charge $55 per form. Allow 7 business days to complete a form.

## Driver Medicals

A driver medical costs $120 and takes 45 minutes. Bring your glasses if you wear them.
