# Parking at Central

## Quay Street Car Park

Patients get 2 hours of free parking when they have their ticket validated at reception. After that parking costs $6 per hour, up to a daily maximum of $30.

## Accessible Parking

There are 6 accessible parking bays on level 1 next to the lift.

## Bikes

Bike racks are at the front entrance.
