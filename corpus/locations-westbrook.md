# Harbourside Westbrook

## Address and Services

Harbourside Westbrook is at 9 Millrace Lane. It offers family medicine and physiotherapy only, and it does not see pediatric patients. Pathology collection is on site from 8:00am to 11:00am, Monday to Friday.

## Opening Hours

Westbrook is open Monday to Thursday from 8:00am to 5:00pm and on Friday from 8:00am to 4:00pm. Westbrook is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

## Community Fee Scale

Westbrook is the only clinic that offers the sliding scale fee. Details are in the insurance and rebates document.
