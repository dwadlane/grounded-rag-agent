# Prescriptions and Repeats

## Repeat Prescriptions

Allow 2 business days for a repeat prescription. Request it through the portal or at the front desk.

## Script Only Requests

If your doctor reviewed you in the last 6 months you can request a repeat without a visit. We charge $20 for a script only request.

## Controlled Medicines

Controlled medicines always need an appointment. We never issue them by phone or message.

## Getting Your Script

We send your e-script by SMS or directly to your pharmacy.
