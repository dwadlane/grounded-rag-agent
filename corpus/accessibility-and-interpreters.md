# Accessibility and Interpreters

## Physical Access

All three clinics have step free entrances and accessible toilets.

## Interpreters

We provide free interpreters in over 40 languages, in person or by phone. Ask for one at least 48 hours before your appointment.

## Hearing and Vision

Central and Northshore reception desks have a hearing loop. Large print forms are available on request.

## Service Animals

Service animals are welcome in all clinics.
