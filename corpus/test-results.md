# Test Results

## When to Expect Results

Blood and urine test results take 2 to 3 business days. Imaging reports take 1 business day.

## How You Get Results

Results appear in the patient portal. We call you only when a result needs action. We do not send results by email.

## Sensitive Results

We discuss sensitive results only in person or on a telehealth call.
