# Child and Family Services

## Pediatric Care

Pediatric care is available at Central and Northshore only. Westbrook does not see pediatric patients.

## Immunization Clinic

The immunization clinic runs at Northshore on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9:00am to 12:00pm, by appointment.

## Baby Weigh-Ins

Free baby weigh-ins run at Central on Wednesdays from 10:00am to 12:00pm. No appointment is needed.

## School and Sports Forms

School and sports forms cost $25 each.
