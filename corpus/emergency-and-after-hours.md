# Emergencies and After Hours

## Emergencies

We are not an emergency service. In an emergency call 911.

## After Hours Nurse Line

Our after hours nurse line is 1-800-555-0142. It is staffed from 6:00pm to 8:00am on weekdays and all weekend. It cannot issue prescriptions.

## Urgent but Not an Emergency

If you need to be seen the same day, call us at 8:00am for a same day appointment.
