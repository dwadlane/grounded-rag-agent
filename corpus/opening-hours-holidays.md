# Holiday Opening Hours

## Public Holidays

Central and Northshore are open from 9:00am to 1:00pm on public holidays, except the days listed in this document. Westbrook is closed on all public holidays.

## Christmas and New Year

All three clinics are closed on December 25, December 26 and January 1. On December 24 and December 31 every clinic closes at 1:00pm.

## Easter

All three clinics are closed on Good Friday and Easter Sunday. Easter Monday is treated as a normal public holiday.

## Announcements

We post holiday hours on our website 14 days before each holiday.
