# Privacy and Medical Records

## Requesting Your Records

Ask for your records in writing through the portal form, or at the front desk with photo ID. We prepare them within 10 business days.

## Copy Fees

The first electronic copy is free. A paper copy costs $25.

## Transfers

We transfer your records to another doctor for free within 5 business days of a signed request.

## Family Access

Parents can access the records of a child under 14. From age 14 we need the teenager's consent.
