# Parking at Northshore and Westbrook

## Northshore

Northshore has 40 free on site parking spaces, including 3 accessible bays.

## Westbrook

Westbrook has 12 on site spaces. When they are full you can use the free Millrace Hall lot, a 2 minute walk away. Street parking nearby has a 2 hour limit.

## Drop Off

Both clinics have a drop off zone at the front door for patients who find walking difficult.
