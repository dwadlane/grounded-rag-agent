# Payment and Billing

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, and cash. We do not accept checks or cryptocurrency. Payment is due at the time of the visit.

## Payment Plans

For balances over $300 we offer an interest free payment plan of up to 6 months. Ask the front desk or email billing@harbourside.example to set one up.

## Billing Disputes

Raise any question about an invoice within 30 days of the invoice date. We reply within 5 business days.

## Receipts

We email an itemized receipt on the day of the visit.
