# Referral Rules

## Specialist Referrals

We issue specialist referrals only after an appointment with one of our doctors. We cannot issue a referral by phone or by message. A specialist referral is valid for 12 months.

## Imaging and Pathology Requests

An imaging request for an X-ray, ultrasound or MRI is valid for 90 days from the date we issue it. A pathology request is valid for 60 days.

## Referrals From Outside Doctors

We accept an outside referral for physiotherapy only if it is signed and dated within the last 12 months.

## Urgent Referrals

For an urgent referral we send the paperwork to the specialist on the same day and tell the patient.
