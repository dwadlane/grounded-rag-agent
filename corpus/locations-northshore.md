# Harbourside Northshore

## Address and Services

Harbourside Northshore is at 220 Beacon Road. It offers family medicine, pediatrics, physiotherapy and the immunization clinic. Pathology collection is not available at Northshore, so patients use Central or Westbrook.

## Opening Hours

Northshore is open Monday to Friday from 8:00am to 6:00pm and on Saturday from 9:00am to 12:00pm. Northshore is closed on Sunday.

## Getting There

Bus route 7 stops at the corner of Beacon Road. The ferry terminal is a 10 minute walk away.
