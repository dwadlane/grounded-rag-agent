# New Patient Registration

## What to Bring

Bring photo ID, your insurance card and a list of your current medicines to your first appointment.

## Arrival Time

Arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit. If you complete the online forms beforehand you can arrive 5 minutes early.

## Children and Guardians

A patient under 16 must come with a parent or guardian to the first visit. A patient who is 16 or 17 can come alone with a signed consent form.

## Registration Fee

There is no registration fee.
