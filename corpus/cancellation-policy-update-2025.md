# Cancellation and Missed Appointment Policy Update

## Notice Required

We need 48 hours notice to cancel any appointment.

## Late Cancellation Fee

A late cancellation fee of $50 applies when we receive less than the required notice.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment with no notice is charged a no show fee of $75.

## Rescheduling

Each appointment can be rescheduled free of charge twice.

## Telehealth Appointments

A telehealth appointment needs 4 hours notice to cancel. A $25 fee applies after that.
