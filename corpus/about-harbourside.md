# About Harbourside Outpatient Clinics

## Who We Are

Harbourside Outpatient Clinics is a group of three primary care and allied health clinics founded in 2011. We provide family medicine, pediatrics, physiotherapy, immunizations and minor procedures. We do not run an emergency department or overnight beds.

## Our Locations

We have three clinics. Harbourside Central is at 14 Quay Street, Harbourside Northshore is at 220 Beacon Road, and Harbourside Westbrook is at 9 Millrace Lane.
