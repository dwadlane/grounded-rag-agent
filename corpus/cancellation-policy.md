# Cancellation and Missed Appointment Policy

## Notice Required

We need 12 hours notice to cancel a family medicine or pediatric appointment. We need 36 hours notice to cancel a physiotherapy appointment or a procedure.

## Late Cancellation Fee

A late cancellation fee of $45 applies when we receive less than the required notice. We waive the fee for your first late cancellation in any 12 months.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment with no notice is charged a no show fee of $80.

## Rescheduling

Each appointment can be rescheduled free of charge three times.

## Telehealth Appointments

A telehealth appointment needs 2 hours notice to cancel. A $20 fee applies after that.
