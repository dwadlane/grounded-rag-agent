# Booking an Appointment

## How to Book

You can book through our online portal at any time. You can also call us from 7:30am to 5:30pm on weekdays. Walk-ins are accepted only at Central on Saturdays.

## Booking Window

You can book up to 90 days ahead.

## Same Day Appointments

We release same day appointments at 8:00am each weekday. They are available by phone only and numbers are limited.

## Choosing a Doctor

You can ask for a specific doctor, although the wait may be longer.
