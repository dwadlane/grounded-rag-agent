# Telehealth Services

## Who Can Use Telehealth

Telehealth is for existing patients who have been seen in person at one of our clinics in the last 12 months. It is for family medicine and physiotherapy follow-ups. Children under 2 need to be seen in person.

## Telehealth Fees

A telehealth call of up to 15 minutes costs $75. A telehealth call of up to 30 minutes costs $130.

## Joining a Call

We email your video link 24 hours before the call and again 15 minutes before it. You can join up to 10 minutes early. The clinician waits 10 minutes and then marks the visit as missed.

## Prescriptions

Telehealth e-scripts are sent to you by SMS. We do not prescribe controlled medicines by telehealth.
