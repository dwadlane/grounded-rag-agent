# Insurance and Rebates

## Accepted Insurers

We bill four insurers directly: Northwind Health, BlueHarbor, Meridian Mutual and Capital Care. For any other insurer you pay at the visit and claim the cost back yourself.

## Sliding Scale Fees

Westbrook offers a sliding scale fee to patients with a household income under $42,000. The fee for a standard visit is reduced to $55. Proof of income is required and stays valid for 12 months.

## Government Programs

We are not enrolled with Medicare or Medicaid, so we cannot bill either program.

## Out of Network Claims

We give every patient an itemized receipt with the service codes needed for an out of network claim.
