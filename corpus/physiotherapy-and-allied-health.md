# Physiotherapy and Allied Health

## Self Referral

You can book physiotherapy at any of our three clinics without a referral.

## Chronic Care Plans

A patient with a chronic care plan from one of our doctors can claim up to 5 physiotherapy sessions per calendar year at the plan rate of $60 per session.

## What to Bring

Wear loose clothing and bring any imaging reports related to your problem.
