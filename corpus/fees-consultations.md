# Consultation Fees

## Standard Fees

A standard family medicine visit of up to 15 minutes costs $95. A long visit of up to 30 minutes costs $160. A pediatric visit costs $110.

## Physiotherapy Fees

A physiotherapy initial assessment of 45 minutes costs $130. A physiotherapy follow-up of 30 minutes costs $90.

## Procedure Fees

Minor procedure fees start at $140. We quote the exact fee before we begin.

## Fee Changes

We review our fees every January 1. New fees apply to visits on or after that date.
