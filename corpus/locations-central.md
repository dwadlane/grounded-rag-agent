# Harbourside Central

## Address and Services

Harbourside Central is at 14 Quay Street, next to Harbour Station. It offers family medicine, pediatrics, physiotherapy, minor procedures and on-site pathology collection.

## Opening Hours

Central is open Monday to Friday from 7:30am to 7:00pm and on Saturday from 8:00am to 1:00pm. Central is closed on Sunday.

## Getting There

Central is a 4 minute walk from Harbour Station. Bus routes 12 and 40 stop outside the front door.
