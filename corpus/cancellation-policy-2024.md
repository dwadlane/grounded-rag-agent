# Cancellation and Missed Appointment Policy

## Notice Required

We need 24 hours notice to cancel any appointment.

## Late Cancellation Fee

A late cancellation fee of $30 applies when we receive less than the required notice.

## Missed Appointments

A missed appointment with no notice is charged a no show fee of $60.

## Rescheduling

You can reschedule an appointment as many times as you like with 24 hours notice.

## Telehealth Appointments

You can cancel a telehealth appointment at any time before the call without a fee.
